Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

ScriptSpark does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party.

On-device AI

Script generation uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). Your prompts and generated scripts are processed entirely on your device and never leave it.

Your Library

Scripts you save are stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data, and it is never uploaded to our servers — we don't operate any servers that store user content.

Optional "Bring Your Own AI" (Pro feature)

If you choose to connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini API key, that key is stored securely in your device's Keychain and is never sent to us. When you use this feature, your prompts are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose, governed by that provider's own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing their policies before use.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store via StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Analytics & Tracking

ScriptSpark does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

ScriptSpark does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally; changes will be reflected on this page.
